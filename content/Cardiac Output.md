Normal: 4-8 L/min
CO =  [[Stroke Volume|SV]] * [[Heart Rate|HR]]

For temporary boost, pace heart rate to 90-110

Invasive measuring:
- Direct Fick Method
	- enter arterial and mixed venous blood gas into calculator
- Dye dilution method
	- inject indocyanine green and measure at set intervals
- Thermodilution method
	- Intermittent bolus
		- inject temperature water and measure change at [[Pulmonary Artery Catheter| PA Catheter]]
	- Continuous cardiac output
		- Filament near [[Pulmonary Artery Catheter|PA Catheter]] proximal port intermittently warms blood
Minimally invasive measuring:
- Thoracic electrical bioimpedance
	- electrodes on base of neck and sides of abdomen measure impedance from flowing blood in thoracic aorta
- TEE
- Pulse contour analysis
