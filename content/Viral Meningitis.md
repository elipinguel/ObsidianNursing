Causes:
- Enteroviruses
- [[herpes symplex virus]]
- [[Varicella zoster virus]]
- [[Human Immunodeficiency Virus|HIV]]

Signs and symptoms:
- Elevated [[Cerebrospinal Fluid|CSF]] protein, but not as much as [[Bacterial Meningitis]]
- ===Normal [[Cerebrospinal Fluid|CSF]] glucose===
- Lymphocytes
- Headache
- Photophobia
- Nuchal regidity
- [[Brudzinski's Sign]]
- [[Kernig's Sign]]
