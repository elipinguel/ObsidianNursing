[[BUN-Creatinine Ratio]] ≥ 20:1 with elevated [[Creatinine]]

From impaired renal blood flow

Causes:
- Hypotension
- #diuretic 

Signs and symptoms:
- [[Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System|RAAS]] activation
	- Causes low [[Fractional Excretion of Sodium]]
- Urine specific gravity > 1.030
- Urine osmolality > 500 mOsm/kg
