Signs and symptoms:
- Tearing, stabbing sensation in back
- Pulse deficit
- [[Aortic Insufficiency|Aortic Regurgitation]]

Treatment:
- Assess distal BP
- Lower HR before BP due to refractory tachycardia with hypotension
- MAP 60-65, SBP 100-120
	- [[Beta Blocker]]
	- [[Calcium Channel Blockers]]
- [[esmolol]]
- [[nicardipine]]
- Endovascular repair
- Surgical aortic repair
	- Maintain spinal perfusion pressures ≤ 10 mm Hg
