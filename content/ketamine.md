- SNS stimulant
- Analgesic
- Amnesiac
- Bronchodilator
	- Great for asthma
- Dissociative once ≥1 mg/kg

>[!tip] Off-Label Use
>Analgesic. Usually paired with #NSAID or #opioid 

Metabolized in liver.
Excreted through kidneys.

Requires quiet environment to reduce delirium

Adverse effects:
- Hypotension if #catecholamine are depleted
- ↑[[Heart Rate|HR]], [[Hypertension|HTN]], ↑[[Cardiac Index]]
- #hepatotoxic
