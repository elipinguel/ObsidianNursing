Malformed placenta releases inflammatory mediators

Prevention:
- [[aspirin]] prior to 16 weeks gestation in high-risk people

Signs and symptoms:
- New-onset [[Hypertension|HTN]]
- Proteinuria
- Visual disturbances
- Liver swelling → right upper quadrant pain
- H/A
- Vomiting
- Oliguria
- Platelet thrombi formation
- Capillary leakage → peripheral and pulmonary edema

Complications:
- Thrombi → [[HELLP Syndrome]]
- Capillary leakage → [[Eclampsia]]

Treatment:
- Supportive care
- Deliver fetus and placenta
