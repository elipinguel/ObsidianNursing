Causes:
- Increased [[Sodium]] intake
	- Hyper-aldosteronism
	- 3% saline
	- Salty food
- Net water loss
	- [[Intrarenal Acute Kidney Injury|Intrarenal AKI]]
	- #loop_diuretic 
	- #osmotic_diuretic
	- [[Diabetes Insipidus]]
	- Hyperglycemia

Signs and symptoms:
- Increased sodium intake
	- Weight gain
	- ↑[[Central Venous Pressure (CVP)|CVP]]
	- Hypertension
- Water loss
	- Thirst
	- Cerebral hemorrhage
	- Hypotension, tachycardia
	- Weight loss

>[!tip]
>Monitor for [[Hypokalemia]]

Treatment:
- Sodium gain:
	- #thiazide_diuretic 
	- Low salt diet
- Water loss:
	- [[Fluid Resuscitation]]

Correct sodium at 0.5 mEq/hr
No more than 10 mEq drop per day
