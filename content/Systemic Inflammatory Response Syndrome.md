>[!warning]
>[[Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services|CMS]] still uses [[Sepsis-1]] guidelines. Clinicians, including this website, use Sepsis-3 guidelines.

Criteria:
- Temperature > 38 C (100.4 F) or < 36 C (96.8 F)
- Heart rate > 90
- Respiratory rate > 20 or PaCO2 < 32 mm Hg
- [[White Blood Cell|WBC]] > 12,000/mm³, [[White Blood Cell|WBC]] < 4,000/mm³, or > 10% immature bands

>[!tip]
>Hypotension is not a sign of SIRS
