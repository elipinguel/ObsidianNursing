Triggers:
- Low blood pressure in kidney
- Sympathetic response
- Low serum [[Sodium]]

Physiology:
1. Kidney releases renin
2. Renin converts angiotensinogen into angiotensin I
3. Angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE) in endothelial cells, the lungs, and the epithelial cells of the kidneys converts angiotensin I into [[Angiotensin II]]
4. Angiotensin II causes kidneys to release [[aldosterone]] and posterior pituitary gland to release [[vasopressin|ADH]]

Retain [[Sodium]] and water at the cost of [[Potassium]]
