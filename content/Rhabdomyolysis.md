Muscle breakdown rapidly increasing serum [[Myoglobin]] and [[Creatinine]], damaging the kidneys

Signs and symptoms:
- [[Creatinine Kinase]] > 5000 units/L
- Myalgia
- Weakness
- Soft tissue edema
- Dark urine
- N/V
- [[Hyperkalemia]]
- [[Hypocalcemia]]
	- Due to muscles releasing [[Phosphate]]
- Low [[BUN-Creatinine Ratio]]
- ↑[[Troponin|Troponin I]] in 50% of people

Treatment:
- Aggressive hydration to prevent [[Acute Kidney Injury|AKI]]
	- 400-1000 mL/hr
	- Titrate to urine output 200 mL/hr
- Foley
- Fasciotomy for compartment syndrome
