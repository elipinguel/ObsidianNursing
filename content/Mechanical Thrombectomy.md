## Anterior Occlusion
Indications:
- Pre-stroke [[Modified Rankin Scale]] 0-1
- Causative large vessel occlusion of [[Internal Carotid Artery]] showed in imaging
- [[National Institute of Health Stroke Scale|NIHSS]] ≥6 and arrived within 6 hrs of symptoms

Can be given up to 24 hrs after symptom onset:
- 0-6 hrs
	- [[Alberta Stroke Program Early CT Score|ASPECT Score]] 3-10
- 6-24 hrs
	- [[Alberta Stroke Program Early CT Score|ASPECT Score]] ≥ 6
- 6-24 hrs
	- ≥80 years old
	- [[Alberta Stroke Program Early CT Score|ASPECT Score]] 3-5
	- Without significant mass effect on imaging

## Posterior Occlusion
Indications:
- Pre-stroke [[Modified Rankin Scale]] 0-1
- Causative large vessel [[Basilar Artery Occlusion]] on imagingshowed in imaging
- [[National Institute of Health Stroke Scale|NIHSS]] ≥10
- [[Alberta Stroke Program Early CT Score|ASPECT Score]] ≥6

Can be given up to 24 hrs after symptom onset.
