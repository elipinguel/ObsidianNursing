Supply ischemia

Types:
- [[ST-segment Elevation Myocardial Infarction|STEMI]]
- [[Non-ST-Segment Elevation Myocardial Infarction]]
- [[Unstable Angina]]

Biomarkers:
- [[Myoglobin]]
- [[Creatinine Kinase-MB]]
- [[Troponin|Troponin T]]
- [[Troponin|Troponin I]]
	- Most specific for MI

Immediate treatment:
1. Oxygen as needed
2. [[aspirin]]
3. [[12 Lead ECG]]
4. Translingual or sublingual [[nitroglycerin]] if chest discomfort continues
