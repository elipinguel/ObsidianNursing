# Adults

Signs:
- LV hypertrophy
- Blurred vision
- H/A
- Confusion
- [[S3 Heart Sound]]
- Crackles

Treatment:
- ==Up to -25% SBP reduction in 1 hr==
- ==Up to -160 SBP reduction in 2-6 hrs==
- [[nitroprusside]]
- [[nitroglycerin]]
- [[nicardipine]]
- #beta_blocker 
	- Avoid in [[cocaine]] and [[methamphetamine]]

May progress into [[Hypertensive Encephalopathy]]

# Pregnancy

Average of at least 2 SBP ≥160 mmHg or DBP ≥100 mmHg at least 15 mins apart using the same limb
