>[!tip]
>Hagen-Poiseuille equation
>$Q=\frac{\pi r^{4} \Delta P}{8 \eta L}$
>tells us that flow volume rate (Q) is increased with shorter (L) and larger bore catheters (r).
>
>Thus large bore peripheral lines are best.

Blood must be warmed due to risk for hypothermia.

>[!warning]
>Only run [[Normal Saline]] with blood products. [[Calcium]] in [[Lactated Ringers|LR]] will counteract anticoagulant effects of [[citrate]]

Do not transfuse:
- [[Platelet]] due to heat causing adhesion to tubing
- [[Cryoprecipitate]] due to rapid infusion damaging clotting proteins
- Granulocyte suspensions due to rapid infusion damaging cells
