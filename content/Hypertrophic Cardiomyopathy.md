Ventricle thickening reduces amount of blood ventricle can hold/push. Also partially occludes aortic valve

> [!warning]
> Avoid vasodilators, inotropes, and #diuretic to prevent left ventricular collapse

Treatment:
- #beta_blocker 
- #calcium_channel_blocker 
- Similar to [[Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction|HFpEF]]
- ICD
