Signs:
- Dyspnea
- Pleuritic chest pain
- Dry cough
- Hemoptysis
- [[Inverted T Wave]] in leads V1-V4
- SᵢQᵢᵢᵢ,Tᵢᵢᵢ pattern
	- S wave in lead I
	- Pathological Q in lead III
	- [[Inverted T Wave]] in lead III
- [[Jugular Vein Distention (JVD)|JVD]]
- Tachypnea
- Asymmetric limb edema
- [[Pulmonary Hypertension]]

Causes:
- VTE
	- Leads to [[Cor Pulmonale]]
- Air or fat
	- Increases capillary permeability, flooding alveoli
- Non-thrombotic
	- Catheters

Diagnostics:
- Positive [[D-Dimer]]
- ==CT angiogram==
	- Gold standard
- V/Q Scan

Types:
- Acute minor
- Subacute massive
	- Heart compensates over time
	- SBP ≥90
	- RV dilation and systolic dysfunction
	- BNP >90 pg/mL
	- S1Q3T3 ECG pattern
- Acute massive
	- Sudden >50% obstruction
	- Severe bradycardia <40 HR
	- Pulselessness
	- Hypotension

Treatment:
- Unfractionated heparin → LMWH, [[Direct Oral Anticoagulants (DOACs)]], or [[warfarin]]
- Thrombolysis
- [[Mechanical Thrombectomy]]
- [[Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation|ECMO]]
- IVC filter
