Heart cannot keep up with workload

Signs and Symptoms:
- Nocturia due to intravascular fluid shifts at night
- Dependent edema
- Left-sided:
	- Cool, clammy extremities
	- [[Pulmonary Edema]]
- Right-sided:
	- [[Jugular Vein Distention (JVD)|JVD]]

Types:
- [[Heart Failure with Reduced Ejection Fraction]]
- [[Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction]]
- [[Heart Failure with Moderately Reduced Ejection Fraction]]

Pathophysiology:
- ↓renal perfussion → [[Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System]] → Fluid overload
- Stretched myocardium → [[Brain Natriuretic Peptide]] → ↓sympathetic response, ↓[[Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System|RAAS]]

Treatment:
- RAAS Blocker
	- #ACE_inhibitor 
	- #ARB 
	- #aldosterone_antagonist
	- Can cause [[Hyperkalemia]] and dehydration
- #loop_diuretic 
	- May cause ototoxicity
- #thiazide_diuretic 
	- May cause [[Hyponatremia]], [[Hypokalemia]]
- #inotrope
- #beta_blocker for maintenance
	- [[metoprolol]]
	- [[carvedilol]]
	- bisoprolol
- [[digoxin]]
	- Stop sodium-potassium pump
		- Stop sodium-calcium pump
			- ↑intracellular [[Calcium]] for ↑[[Cardiac Contractility|Contractility]]
