Risk factor management:
- Mediterranean diet
- Limit alcohol
	- Men: 2 drinks daily
	- Women: 1 drink daily
- Stop sympathomimetics
- Treat sleep disorders
- ==HTN management==
- DM management
- [[Atrial Fibrillation|A-fib]] management

- SpO2 > 94%
- SBP < 185
- BG 140-180

Medications:
- Antiplatelet within 24 hrs of admission and at D/C
	- 81 mg [[aspirin]]
	- [[clopidogrel]]
	- Aggrenox
		- Halve dose if headache
- Anticoagulants
	- [[warfarin]]
	- [[Direct Oral Anticoagulants|DOACs]]
		- Wait 4-14 days before starting in ischemic stroke with [[Atrial Fibrillation|A-fib]]
	- [[heparin]]
- Antihypertensives
	- #diuretic 
	- #beta_blocker
	- #ACE_inhibitor
	- #ARB
	- #calcium_channel_blocker
		- [[nimodipine]] showed better outcomes with stroke
- [[Statins]]
- #Anticonvulsant if decreased [[Level of Consciousness|LOC]]
	- [[phenytoin]]
	- [[levetiracetam]]
- Glycemic control
- Antispasmodics
	- [[baclofen]]
- [[Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors|SSRIs]]
- [[methylphenidate]] to stimulate
- [[haloperidol]] for delirium

Functional outcomes:
- Patient needs to want to regain function
- Robotic therapy
