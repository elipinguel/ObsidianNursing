[[BUN-Creatinine Ratio]] 10-15:1 but [[Creatinine]] and [[Blood Urea Nitrogen|BUN]] are elevated

Glomerulus damage

Causes:
- Hypoperfusion over time
- Kidney damage
- [[Acute Tubular Necrosis|ATN]]

Signs and symptoms:
- Urine specific gravity < 1.015
- Urine osmolality < 500 mOsm/kg
- Urine sodium > 10-15 mEq/L
- Urine casts from tubule damage
- Proteinuria
	- Can causes 3rd spacing
- Hematuria

Phases:
1. Oliguric
	1. 5 days - 2 weeks
	2. [[Fluid Overload]]
	3. ↑serum [[Blood Urea Nitrogen|BUN]], ↑serum [[Creatinine]], ↑serum [[Potassium]]
2. Diuretic
	1. 7-14 days
	2. ↓urine specific gravity
	3. Electrolyte loss
	4. ↑serum [[Blood Urea Nitrogen|BUN]], ↑serum [[Creatinine]]
3. Recovery
	1. Up to 2 years
