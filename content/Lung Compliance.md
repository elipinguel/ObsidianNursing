Inverse relationship with [[Lung Elasticity]]

Increased by:
- [[Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)|COPD]]
	- Easy to get air in but hard to get out due to low [[Lung Elasticity]]
- Aging
Decreased by:
- Fibrosis
- [[Pulmonary Edema]]
- Surfactant deficiency
- ↑Abdominal pressure
	- Pregnancy
	- Obesity
	- Tight pants
	- Retroperitoneal bleed

Types:
- Dynamic
	- Compliance with airflow on a ventilator
	- Measured by peak inspiratory pressure
- Static
	- Compliance without airflow
	- Measured with plateau pressure during inspiratory pause
	- Measures [[Lung Elasticity]]
