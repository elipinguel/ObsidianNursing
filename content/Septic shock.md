Criteria:
- [[Sepsis]] criteria
- Hypotension requiring vasopressors to maintain [[Mean Arterial Pressure|MAP]] > 65
- [[Lactic Acid]] > 2 mmol/L despite [[Fluid Resuscitation]]

Warm stage overlaps with normal [[Sepsis]]

Usually caused by [[Gram-Negative Bacteria]]

Stages:
1. Warm stage up to 72 hrs
	1. Signs and symptoms:
		1. Confusion
		2. Increased [[Cardiac Output|CO]]
		3. Increased respiratory rate
			1. Good [[Arterial Blood Gas|ABG]]
		4. Good [[Urine Output]]
		5. Vasodilation
			1. Causes warm, flushed skin
	2. Treatment:
		1. [[Fluid Resuscitation]]
2. Cold stage
	1. Signs and symptoms:
		1. Hypotension
		2. Venous pooling
	2. Treatment:
		1. [[Fluid Resuscitation]]
		2. Vasopressors
			1. Start with [[norepinephrine]]
		3. Drain abscess, debride necrotic tissue, remove infected devices within 6-12 hrs
		4. #corticosteroid for refractory septic shock with high-dose vasopressors
		5. [[Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy|CRRT]] for refractory [[Hyperkalemia]], acidosis, or [[Fluid Overload]]

>[!warning]
>[OPTPRESS](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00134-025-07910-4) trial showed that a higher MAP goal in Japanese people in septic shock with chronic [[Hypertension|HTN]] had an increase in mortality. Maintain normal MAP goal (≥65)
