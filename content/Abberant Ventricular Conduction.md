Ventricular conduction disturbance caused by R-R interval changing without compensatory change in refractory period length

Unlike [[Premature Ventricular Contraction|PVC]]s, abberantly conducted beats have a preceding [[P-Wave]]

![[Abberant Ventricular Conduction.png]]

Usually [[Right Bundle Branch Block]] morphology when HR < 100

Usually [[Left Bundle Branch Block]] morphology when HR > 100
