Normal: 7-15 mm Hg
Intracranial hypertension 15-20 mm Hg
Pahtologic: >20 mm Hg

==Earliest sign in high ICP is change in mentation==

Lowering ICP:
1. HOB 30°
   Head midline. Logroll
   To keep jugular vein patent
   Decrease stimuli to prevent sympathetic storming
   Lidocaine prior to [[Endotracheal Tube]] suctioning
2. Increase sedation and analgesic
   PaCO2 35-38 mm Hg
   #antiepiletptic prophylaxis for 7 days max
   [[mannitol]] boluses
   Hypertonic saline
	1. May cause pontine infarct
		1. Can cause [[Basilar Artery Occlusion]]
3. PaCO2 32-35
   #paralytic 
   Raise [[Cerebral Perfusion Pressure|CPP]] with fluids, vasopressors, inotrops
4. [[phenobarbital]] coma
   Surgical decompression
   Mild hypothermia

>[!tip]
>Monitor for [[Diabetes Insipidus|DI]] and [[Syndrome of Inappropriate Antidiuretic Hormone|SIADH]]

Avoid:
- Acidosis
	- Acts on ion channels to relax smooth muscles and vasodilate
- ↑CO2
	- Vasodilation to remove more CO2
- ↓O2
	- Vasodilation to increase oxygen
- Hypotonic solutions
- Protein deficiency
- Restraints
	- Straining against restraints increases ICP
- Bending legs or hips
	- To keep femoral vein patent

Compliant brain can tolerate changes in intracranial volume without drastically changing pressure
