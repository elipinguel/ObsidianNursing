Pericardial pressure collapses atria

Signs:
- Beck's triad (usually show 1-2 signs, not all 3)
	- [[Jugular Vein Distention|JVD]]
	- Muffled heart sounds
	- Hypotension
- [[Pulsus Paradoxus]]
- ↑[[Central Venous Pressure|CVP]]
- Wide mediastinum and large heart on x-ray
- Narrow [[Pulse Pressure]]
- Equalization of right and left heart pressures
- [[Pulsus Alternans]]
- [[Electrical Alternans]]
- Inferior vena cava dilation

Treatment:
- pericardiocentesis
- [[Fluid Resuscitation]]
- Avoid mechanical ventilation
