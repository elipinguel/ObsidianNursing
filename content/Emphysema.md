Type of [[Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)|COPD]] with damaged, less elastic alveoli

Causes:
- Alpha1-antitrypsin deficiency
- Smoking

Signs and symptoms:
- Some chest pain from cough and chest tightness
	- Should not be sudden or different from normal
- Accessory muscles
- Wheezing
- Pink skin
- SpO2 88-92%

Complications:
- Right [[Heart Failure]]
