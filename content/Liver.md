Functions:
- [[Kupffer cell]] detoxify blood
- Produces:
	- Bile
		- Conjugates [[Bilirubin]] and excretes into bile
	- Amino acids
	- [[albumin]]
	- Prothrombin
	- Fibrinogen
	- cholesterol
	- triglycerides
- Converts glucose into glycogen
- Converts ammonia into urea
- Stores fat-soluble vitamins, [[Vitamin B12]], iron, and copper
