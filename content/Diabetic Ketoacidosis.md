---
aliases:
  - DKA
---
Causes:
- Insulin deficiency causes [[Ketogenesis]]

Signs and symptoms:
- Blood glucose 300-800 mg/dL
- Dehydration
- Acidosis
- Anion gap greater than 12
- Elevated [[beta-hydroxybutyric acid]]

Treatment:
- Insulin
	- [[Potassium]] must be normal or high lest risk [[Hypokalemia]]
- [[Fluid Resuscitation]]
	- Add dextrose when BG 250-300 mg/dL
	- Transition to hypotonic fluids for cellular hydration
- Stop insulin drip 2 hrs after subcutaneous long-acting insulin
