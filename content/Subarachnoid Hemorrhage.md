- "Worst headache of life" (WHOL) ^af0169
- [[Kernig's Sign]] and [[Brudzinski's Sign]]

- Coil or clip aneurysm
- Glue [[Arteriovenous Malformation]]
- [[dobutamine]]

- [[epinephrine]]
- [[milrinone]]
