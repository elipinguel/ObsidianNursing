Measure of ventilation

Normal: 35-45 mm Hg

Increased by:
- Hypermetabolic state
	- Fever
	- Muscle use
	- Pain
- Diminished respiratory function
	- Respiratory depression
	- [[Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)|COPD]]
- ↑[[Cardiac Output|CO]] due to more blood going to lungs
- [[sodium bicarbonate]]
Decreased by:
- Hypometabolic state
	- Hypothermia
	- Metabolic acidosis
- Hyperventilation
- Bronchospasm
- Mucous plug
- Hypotension
- Cardiac arrest
- [[Pulmonary Embolism|PE]]
- [[epinephrine]]
