[[Recombinant Tissue Plasminogen Activator (rtPA)|rtPA]]

Dosing:
- 0.25 mg/kg
- Max: 25 mg
- One-time bolus
- Start IV within 4.5 hrs of onset

Best for large vessel obstruction or mechanical embolectomy

 Post-adminsitration neuro check every:
- 15 mins for 2 hrs
- 30 mins for 6 hrs
- 60 mins or 16 hrs
