## Definition
Ratio of hazard rates.

## Formula
$$
HR = \frac{Experimental Event Rate}{Control Event Rate}
$$

## Interpretation
- HR = 1
	- Event rates are the same in both groups.
- HR > 1
	- Event rates are higher in experimental group.
- HR < 1
	- Event rates are higher in control group.

## Example
In a drug clinical trial measuring mortality, HR = mortality in treatment group divided by mortality in control group. If HR is 0.4, then patients were 60% less likely to die than patients in the control group.
