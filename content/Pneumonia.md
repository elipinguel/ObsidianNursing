>[!warning]
>Do not give antitussive

Community-Acquired:
- Diagnosed with/ 72 hrs of hospitalization
Hospital-Acquired:
- Generally harder to treat
Aspiration

Signs and symptoms:
- Bronchial consolidations
- Crackles

Sputum:
- Rust color
	- *Streptococcus pneumoniae*
- Green color
	- *Pseudomonas*, *Haemophilus*, *Pneumococcal*
- Foul smell
	- From anaerobic bacteria

Treatment:
- Expectorant or mucolytic
