Has:
- Expiratory positive airway pressure (EPAP) = [[Positive End-Expiratory Pressure|PEEP]] = [[Continuous Positive Airway Pressure|CPAP]]
	- Max 10-15 cm H2O
	- ↑→ ↑O2
- Inspiratory positive airway pressure (IPAP)
	- ↑ → Lower CO2, 
	- Can also ↑O2
	- ↑ → more abdominal distention → vomiting

Usually starts at 10/5 cm H2O
Adjust for [[Tidal Volume|TV]] of 6-8 mL/kg

Contraindications:
- No respiratory drive or agonal breathing
- Cannot protect airway
- Cannot sit up
- Hypotension
- Bad mask seal
	- No teeth
	- Beard
	- Head trauma
- [[Closed Head Injury]]
- Upper [[Gastrointestinal Bleed]]
- Nausea and vomiting
- Chest trauma
