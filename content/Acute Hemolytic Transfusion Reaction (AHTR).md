Occurs within 5 mins to 24 hrs of transfusion

Early signs:
- Fever ≥100.4 F
- ↑temperature by 1.8-3.6 F
- ↑HR
- IV site pain
- Chills
- Dyspnea
- Chest or back pain
- Anxiety
- Hematuria
Late signs:
- ↓BP
- [[Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC)|DIC]]

Treatment:
- Fluids to minimize hemolysis renal damage
