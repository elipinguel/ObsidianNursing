13.3% of inferior occlusion [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]]s present as Aslanger pattern and are often misdiagnosed as [[Non-ST-Segment Elevation Myocardial Infarction (NSTEMI)|NSTEMI]]

Criteria:
- ST elevation in lead III but NOT II or aVF
- ST depression in any of leads V4-6 with at least terminally positive T wave
	- Only either V2 or V3 have ST elevation
- ST elevation in V1 is larger than in V2/V3 (whichever has elevation)

Treatment: emergent [[Cardiac Catheterization|PCI]]
