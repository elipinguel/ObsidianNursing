Low of [[vasopressin|ADH]]

Causes:
- TBI
- Hypoxic encephalopathy
- Meningitis
- [[phenytoin]]
- Tumors

Signs and symptoms:
- Polyuria 20 L/day
- Specific gravity < 1.005
- Urine [[Osmolality]] < 200 mOsm/L
- Serum [[Osmolality]] > 295
- Serum [[Sodium]] > 145
- Polydipsia 
- ↑[[Blood Urea Nitrogen|BUN]]

Treatment:
- [[vasopressin|ADH]]
- [[Fluid Resuscitation]]
