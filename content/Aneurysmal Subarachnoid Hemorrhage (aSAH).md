[[Subarachnoid Hemorrhage]] where an aneurysm ruptured. Usually in Circle of Willis

Degenerative or congenital

Bleeds → clots → clot dissolves in 3 days

Risk factors:
- Female
- HTN
- Smoking
- Loss body mass index
- Cocaine, methamphetamines

Treatment:
- Prophylactic [[phenytoin]] or [[levetiracetam]] for 7 days
- Coil or clip
- Daily [[Transcranial Doppler]] for vasospasm

Complications:
- Absorptive hydrocephalus → [[Ventriculostomy]] for 7-10 days → lumbar drain for 7-10 days → ventriculoperitoneal shunt
- Vasospasm peaks in 7-10 days
	- Manage HTN and euvolemia
	- Balloon angioplasty
	- Intraarterial vasodilators
