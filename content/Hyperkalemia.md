[[Potassium]] > 5.5 mEq/L

Causes:
- Burn
- Crush injury
- [[Acute Kidney Injury|AKI]], [[Chronic Kidney Disease|CKD]]

Signs and Symptoms:
- Weakness
- Paresthesia
- Hypoactive reflexes
- Abdominal cramps
- Diarrhea
- Vomiting
- ==ECG changes==
	1. [[Peaked T Waves]]
	2. Prolonged PR interval
	3. Absence of P waves
	4. Wide QRS

Treatment starts when ECG changes:
- Non-life threatening:
	- [[polystyrene sulfonate]]
	- [[sodium zirconium cyclosilicate]]
	- [[patiromer]]
	- inhaled [[albuterol]]
- Life-threatening:
	- [[Calcium]] to narrow QRS and stabilize myocardium
	- IV insulin + D50 and/or [[sodium bicarbonate]]
	- 3% saline
	- #loop_diuretic
	- [[Dialysis]]
