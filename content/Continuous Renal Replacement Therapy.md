>[!tip]
>Remember indications with "A E I O U"

Indications:
- **A**cid-base imbalance
- **E**lectrolyte imbalance
- **I**ntoxication
- **O**verload
- **U**remia

Method:
- Diffusion
	- Moves electrolytes
- Convection
	- Use fluids to drag solutes out
		- For large molecules
	- Higher risk for filter clot
- Adsorption
	- For cytokines in sepsis

Types:
- Slow Continuous Ultrafiltration (SCUF)
	- Fluid removal only
- Continuous veno-venous hemofiltration (CVVH)
	- Uses convection
- Continuous veno-venous hemodialysis
	- Uses diffusion
- Continuous veno-venous hemodiafiltration
	- Uses convection and diffusion
