> [!warning]
> Must have stable [[Potassium]]

High potassium makes it less effective

- Toxic when >1 ng/mL
- Treat toxicity when ECG changes

Indications:
- [[Atrial Fibrillation|A-fib]]
- [[Heart Failure with Reduced Ejection Fraction|HFrEF]]

Stops sodium-potassium pump → Stop sodium-calcium pump → ↑intracellular [[Calcium]] for ↑[[Cardiac Contractility|Contractility]]
