1. Decreased glucose metabolism
2. Adipose tissue releases triglycerides
3. Triglycerides hydrolyzed into free fatty acids and glycerol
4. Liver beta-oxidizes free fatty acids into acetyl-CoA
5. Acetyl-CoA production exceeds liver's oxidative capacity in the citric acid cycle
6. Liver converts excess acetyl-CoA into ketone bodies, primarily beta-hydroxybutyrate
7. [[beta-hydroxybutyric acid]] is the conjugate acid, causing acidosis
8. Ketone bodies accumulate in the blood and are excreted in ketonuria
