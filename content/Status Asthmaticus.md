[[Asthma]] attack that is resistant to #bronchodilator

Triggers:
- #NSAID 

Signs and symptoms:
- Dehydration
	- From high respiratory rate
- Loss of breath sounds

Treatment:
- High flow oxygen
- Intubation
	- Permissive hypercapnia 50-60 mm Hg and acidosis initially
- #beta-2_antagonist 
- #corticosteroid
- Oxygen
- Continuous [[albuterol]]
- [[Magnesium]]
- Helium
- Inhaled anesthetics
