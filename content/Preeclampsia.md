Abnormal invasion of endometrium by trophoblasts → thick-walled and constricted spiral arteries w/ nerve supply → inadequate placental blood flow → release of vascular irritants

Can occur postpartum

Can develop into [[Eclampsia]] or [[Hemolysis Elevated Liver enzymes Low Platelet Syndrome|HELLP Syndrome]]

Criteria:
- BP
	- SBP ≥140 or DBP ≥90 on 2 occasions at least 4 hrs apart after 20 weeks gestation in previously normal BP
	- SBP ≥160 or DBP ≥110
- Proteinuria
	- ≥300 mg in 24-hour urine
	- Protein/creatinine ratio ≥0.3 mg/dL
	- Urine dipstick proteinuria 2+
		- Prefer not to use this method

Preeclampsia with severe features requires at least one of the following:
- SBP ≥160 or DBP ≥110 on 2 occasions at least 4 hrs apart
- Thrombocytopenia <100,000 / microliter
- Creatinine >1.1 mg/dL or doubling of baseline serum creatinine
- Liver transaminase concentration twice the upper limit of normal
- Pulmonary edema
- New-onset headache unresponsive to interventions
- Visual symptoms

Prevention:
- 81 mg [[aspirin]] prior to 16 weeks gestation in high-risk people

Signs and symptoms:
- New-onset [[Hypertension|HTN]]
- Proteinuria
- Visual disturbances
- Liver swelling → right upper quadrant pain
- H/A
- Vomiting
- Oliguria
- Platelet thrombi formation
- Capillary leakage → peripheral and pulmonary edema
- Vasospasms → hypoxia, edema, hypovolemia

Complications:
- Thrombi → [[Hemolysis Elevated Liver enzymes Low Platelet Syndrome]]
- Capillary leakage → [[Eclampsia]]

Treatment:
- Supportive care
- Deliver fetus and placenta
