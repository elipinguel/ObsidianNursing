Criteria:
- QRS ≥ 0.12 seconds
- rSR' pattern (M-shape) and [[Inverted T Wave]]/depressed T waves in V1-V3
- Broad S-wave longer than R-wave in lateral leads (I, aVL, V5, V6)

Signs and symptoms:
- Delay in right ventricular contraction

Treatment:
- [[Pacemaker]]
