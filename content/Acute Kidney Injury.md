Definition:
- [[Creatinine]] that is 1.5 times greater than baseline in ≤ 7 days **OR**
- [[Creatinine]] >0.3 mg/dL (26.5 µmol/L) in 48 hrs **OR**
- [[Urine Output]] <0.5 mL/kg/hr for 6 hrs

>[!warning]
>[[Creatinine]] is a late biomarker

Types:
- [[Prerenal Acute Kidney Injury|Prerenal AKI]]
- [[Intrarenal Acute Kidney Injury|Intrarenal AKI]]
- [[Postrenal Acute Kidney Injury|Postrenal AKI]]
