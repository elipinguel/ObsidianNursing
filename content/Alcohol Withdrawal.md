Alcohol binds to GABA receptors, causing compensatory rise in glutamate. Withdrawal causes drop in GABA but maintains rise in glutamate.

Signs and symptoms:
- Seizures
- Anxiety/Agitation

Treatment:
- #benzodiazepine bind to GABA receptors
	- [[lorazepam]]
	- [[chlordiazepoxide]]
	- [[diazepam]]
- [[gabapentin]] is a GABA-A analogue
- #alpha-2_agonist decrease [[norepinephrine]] release, minimizing symptoms
	- [[dexmedetomidine]]
	- [[clonidine]]
- #beta-1_antagonist and #beta-2_antagonist minimize symptoms
	- [[propranolol]]
- Quiet environment
- Limit mechanical restraints
- [[thiamine]] for Wernicke's encephalopathy
- Multivitamins with folate
- Correct electrolytes
