Should be started within 24-48 hrs of admission if:
- Functioning GI
-  Cannot ingest orally
- Initiate if MAP ≥ 60 but do not discontinue until MAP < 50

Benefits over [[Parenteral Nutrition]]
- Stimulate immune barrier function
- Reduce Gi atrophy
- Maintain gut mucosa
- More complete nutrition
- Cheaper

Contraindications:
- Non-healing, high-output intestinal fistula
- Bowel obstruction
- Supine
- Severe GI inflammation

Little evidence to check [[Gastric Residual Volume]]

Do not stop feeding when turning. Maintain HOB 10 degrees if needed
