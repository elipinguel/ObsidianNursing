Originating from a 1991 consensus on the definition of [[Sepsis]] and [[Systemic Inflammatory Response Syndrome|SIRS]]. Still used by [[Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services|CMS]] despite clinicians using Sepsis-3 now.

Severe sepsis requires these within 6 hrs of each other:
- ≥2 SIRS:
	- RR > 20
	- HR > 90
	- Temperature >100.9 F (38.3 C) or <96.8 F (36 C)
	- WBC >12,000, <4,000, or Bands >10%
- ≥1 signs of organ dysfunction:
	- Lactic acid >2
	- Hypotension
	- Creatinine >2
	- Urine output <0.5 mL/kg/hr for 2 hrs
	- Bilirubin >2 mg/dL
	- Platelet <100,000
	- INR >1.5
	- aPTT >60
	- BiPAP, CPAP, or intubation

>[!info]
>If initially hypotensive or lactic acid ≥4, start crystalloid fluid bolus of 30 mL/kg

Septic shock requires:
- Severe sepsis
- Either:
	- Lactic acid ≥4
	- Hypotension within 1 hr after crystalloid fluid bolus completion.

Start vasopressors within 6 hrs of septic shock.
