Every shock except [[Neurogenic Shock]] has 3 similar stages:
1.  Compensatory stage
	1. Decrease in [[Cardiac Output|CO]]
	2. Baroreceptors trigger sympathetic response
2. Progressive stage
	1. Vasodilation due to lactic acidosis
3. Refractory stage

Types:
- [[Septic shock]]
- [[Cardiogenic Shock]]
- [[Neurogenic Shock]]
- [[Anaphylactic Shock]]

| Shock         | Preload | Afterload | Cardiac Output |
| ------------- | ------- | --------- | -------------- |
| Cardiogenic   | ↑       | ↑         | ↓              |
| Hypovolemic   | ↓       | ↑         | ↓              |
| Septic (warm) | ↓       | ↓         | ↑              |
| Septic (cold) | ↓       | ↑         | ↓              |
| Anaphylactic  | ↓       | ↓         | ↓              |
