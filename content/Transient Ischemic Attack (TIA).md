Increases risk for stroke after 3 days or 3 months

Cause:
- Carotid stenosis

Symptoms:
- Visual disturbances

Treatment:
- [[Computed Tomography (CT)|CT]] within 24 hrs
- [[Carotid Endarterectomy]]
- Carotid stent if multiple comorbidities
- after 1 day

Complications:
- Labile BP
- Dysphagia from CN damage
- Hyperperfusion syndrome
	- BP control
	- antiepileptic
