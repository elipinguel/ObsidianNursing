- Not VTE prophylaxis

Contraindications:
- [[Thyrotoxicosis]] due to releasing thyroxine from binding sites

Toxicity treatment:
- Fluids to dilute and promote renal excretion
- [[sodium bicarbonate]] to alkalinize urine and trap acidic aspirin metabolites in the urine
