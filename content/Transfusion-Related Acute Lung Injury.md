- Rare
- Donor antibodies target recipient's WBCs in the lungs
- Within 6 hrs after transfusion

Signs:
- Cyanosis
- Non-cardiogenic [[Pulmonary Edema]]
	- No signs of [[Fluid Overload]]
- White out infiltrates
- Hypotension

>[!warning]
>Diuretics can WORSEN hypotension

Treatment:
- Supportive care
