- More insulin than [[Diabetic Ketoacidosis|DKA]]
- Usually in Type 2 DM
- Develops over days to weeks

Signs and symptoms:
- Blood glucose > 600 mg/dL
- Polyuria → oliguria
	- osmotic diuresis into hypovolemia
- Severe dehydration → AMS, [[Azotemia]]
- Serum [[Osmolality]] > 330 mOsm/L due to dehydration

Treatment:
- [[Fluid Resuscitation]]
	- Add dextrose when BG 250-300 mg/dL
	- Transition to hypotonic fluids for cellular hydration
- Insulin
