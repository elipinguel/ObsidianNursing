> [!tip]
> Keep turning oximeter knob until it can no longer physically turn. Will increase flow rate past 15 L/min


Set up:
- Hyperoxygenate
	- Non-invasive ([[Bilevel Positive Airway Pressure|BiPAP]], [[Heated Humidified High-Flow Nasal Cannula]], nasal cannula) is more effective than bag-valve-mask ventilation for intubation
- Position patient
	- Sniffing Position ![[Intubation Sniffing Position.png]]
	- Ramped Position
	  ![[Intubation Ramped Position.png]]
	- Semi-Fowlers
- If hypovolemic, [[Fluid Resuscitation]] before intubating
- Have pressor ready for induction hypotension
- Induce with [[ketamine]] with [[etomidate]]

>[!warning]
>[[midazolam]] and [[propofol]] have greater risk of hemodynamic instability
