Degree:
1. Epidermis only
2. Upper dermis
	1. Extremely painful
3. Subcutaneous
	1. Pain receptors dead; No pain
4. No skin
	1. No pain

[[Hyperbaric Oxygen]] within first 48-72 hrs to improve wound healing

Fluid resuscitation for 2nd, 3rd, and 4th degree:
- 4 mL * burn surface area * weight in kg of [[Lactated Ringers|LR]]
- 50% given over 8 hrs
- Next 50% given over 16 hrs
	- Stop or give [[albumin]] with signs of fluid overload
