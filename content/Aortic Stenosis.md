Restricted aortic artery flow

Auscultation:
- Holosystolic midsystolic ejection murmur
	- Systolic [[Heart Murmur]]
	- Medium pitch crescendo-decrescendo noise
- S2 is more diminished the more severe it is

Signs and symptoms:
- Narrow [[Pulse Pressure]]
- Weak pulses
- ==ASH Triad==
	- Angina
	- Syncope
	- [[Heart Failure]]

[[Arterial Line]]'s waveform:
- Sloped systolic upstroke
- Small
- Undefined dicrotic notch 

![[Arterial Waveform with Aortic Stenosis.png]]

Treatment:
- Care with vasodilator
- #statin 
- [[Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement|TAVR]]
