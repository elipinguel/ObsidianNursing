>[!tip]
>Remember indications with "A E I O U"

Indications:
- **A**cid-base imbalance
- **E**lectrolyte imbalance
- **I**ntoxication
- **O**verload
- **U**remia

Catheter must be ≥11 Fr
Placement preferences in order:
1. Right [[Internal Jugular Vein]]
2. Femoral vein
3. Left [[Internal Jugular Vein]]
4. Subclavian vein

Method:
- Diffusion
	- Moves electrolytes
- Convection
	- Uses pre-filter fluids to drag solutes out
		- For large molecules e.g. [[Myoglobin]]
	- Higher risk for filter clot
- Adsorption
	- For cytokines in [[Sepsis]]
	- Due to negative charge of hemofilter
- Ultrafiltration (UF)
	- Fluid removal

Types:
- Slow Continuous Ultrafiltration (SCUF)
	- Fluid removal only
- Continuous veno-venous hemofiltration (CVVH)
	- Uses convection
- Continuous veno-venous hemodialysis (CVVHD)
	- Uses diffusion
	- For electrolyte correction
- Continuous veno-venous hemodiafiltration (CVVHDF)
	- Uses convection and diffusion
	- Can do everything the other types can do

Effluent should be 20-35 mL/kg/hr. Little efficacy below or above

~179 mL of CRRT tubing

Transmembrane Pressure (TMP):
- Pressure across filter membrane to move into effluent
- Increases as filter degrades
	- If it increases without increase in delta pressure, then filter is NOT clotting

Delta Pressure:
- Pressure gradient before and after filter
- Increases if filter is clotting
	- TMP will also increase

Troubleshooting Clotted Filter:
- Increase pre-filter fluid rate to reduce hematocrit
- Minimum of 200 mL/hr post-filter fluid to prevent deaeration chamber clotting
- 5000 unit [[heparin]] prime
	- Heparin adheres to filter and will not reach the patient
- [[citrate]] as pre-filter fluid


- If pre-filter, dialysate, or post-filter lines are clamped or kinked, patient will lose more fluid than intended.
- If effluent line is clamped or kinked, patient will gain fluid.

If getting flow error from fluid bags, consider spiking bag instead of using Luer lock

Effluent bag should be changed within 6 days

If deaeration chamber alarms with visible foam, raise level until foam is above door. Then lower back down.
