# Pregnancy

Chronic hypertension diagnosed if [[Hypertensive Crisis#Pregnancy]]:
- Prior to pregnancy OR
- Prior to 20 weeks gestation OR
- Diagnosed antenatally and not resolved postpartum

Chronic hypertension w/ superimposed [[Preeclampsia]]:
- Chronic hypertension AND
- ≥1 of the following:
	- Exacerbation of previously well-controlled hypertension
	- New-onset proteinuria or worsening proteinuria
	- Thrombocytopenia <100,000 / microliter
	- Creatinine >1.1 mg/dL or doubling of baseline serum creatinine
	- Liver transaminase concentration twice the upper limit or normal
	- Pulmonary edema
	- New-onset headache unresponsive to interventions
	- Visual symptoms
