[[Alpha-2 Receptor]] on [[Reticular Activating System (RAS)]]

Choice sedative when [[fentanyl]] is ineffective

Puts patient to sleep. Does NOT sedate

Complications:
- Hypertension or hypotension
- Bradycardia
- [[Tachyphylaxis]] after 24 hrs
