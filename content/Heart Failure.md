Heart cannot keep up with workload

Signs and Symptoms:
- Nocturia due to intravascular fluid shifts at night
- Dependent edema
- Left-sided:
	- Cool, clammy extremities
	- [[Pulmonary Edema]]
- Right-sided:
	- [[Jugular Vein Distention (JVD)|JVD]]

Types:
- [[Heart Failure with Reduced Ejection Fraction]]
- [[Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction]]
- [[Heart Failure with Moderately Reduced Ejection Fraction]]

Pathophysiology:
- ↓renal perfussion → [[Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System]] → [[Fluid Overload]]
- Stretched myocardium → [[Brain Natriuretic Peptide|BNP]] → ↓sympathetic response, ↓[[Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System|RAAS]]

Treatment:
- [[Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy]]
- #loop_diuretic 
	- May cause ototoxicity
- #thiazide_diuretic 
	- May cause [[Hyponatremia]], [[Hypokalemia]]
- #inotrope
	- [[digoxin]]
- -2 L/day or -1 kg/24 hrs

>[!tip]
>If resistant to diuretics, switch to continuous IV diuretic i.e. [[bumetanide]] due to rapid renal adaptation.
