Criteria:
- QRS ≥ 1.2 seconds
	- If < 1.2 seconds then Incomplete Left Bundle Branch Block
- Dominant S wave in V1
- Broad, monophasic R wave in lateral leads (I, aVL, V5, V6)
	- Can be notched, M-shaped, monophasic, or RS complex
- No Q wave in lateral leads
- Start of initial r to peak of R wave > 0.06 seconds

Deep S waves may cause ST elevation and is not necessarily indicative of ischemia
