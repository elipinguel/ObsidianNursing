Causes:
- [[Portal Hypertension]]

Treatment:
- Intubate if vomiting
- [[octreotide]]
- [[vasopressin]]
- Antibiotics
	- Reduces re-bleed since infection causes endogenous heparin release
- Slow [[Fluid Resuscitation]]
- Endoscopy
	- Band ligation preferred
- [[Transjugular Intrahepatic Portosystemic Shunt]]
- Sengstaken-Blakemore Tube
- Sodium restriction
- 1.2-1.5 g/kg/day protein
- Thiamine, folic acid, vitamin B6
