- From atherosclerosis with thrombus that partially occludes
- No biomarker elevation in 2 blood samples at least 6 hrs apart AND transient or no ECG changes

Types:
- Rest angina
	- episodes at rest and last >20 mins
- New-onset severe angina
	- Present for <2 months and limits ADLs
- Increasing angina
	- Increasing severity, duration, or frequency

Signs and symptoms:
- ST depression

Treatment:
- coronary angiography or stress test
	- delay diagnostic angiography up to 48 hrs
- [[Cardiac Catheterization|PCI]], [[Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting|CABG]], or medical management

Progresses into [[Non-ST-Segment Elevation Myocardial Infarction|NSTEMI]]
