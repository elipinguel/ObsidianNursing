Mean [[Pulmonary Artery Pressure|PAP]] ≥25 mm Hg at rest

Causes:
- Left [[Heart Failure]]
- [[Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)|COPD]]
- Hypoxia
- [[Pulmonary Embolism|PE]]

Pathophysiology:
- Increase in vasoconstrictor mediators
- Reduction in NO and [[Prostacyclin]]
- Remodeling of pulmonary artery vessel wall

Signs and symptoms:
- ↑[[Pulmonary Artery Pressure|PAP]], ↑[[Central Venous Pressure|CVP]]
- Loud pulmonary S2 heart sound
	- Due to increased pressure

Treatment:
- Reduce salt and fluid intake
- Avoid pregnancy
- Avoid smoking
- Avoid excessive exercise
- Prostacyclin analogues
	- [[epoprostenol]]
	- [[treprostinil]]
	- [[selexipag]]
- Endothelin receptor antagonists
	- bosentan
	- macitentan
	- ambrisentan
- Phosphodiesterase inhibitor
	- [[sildenafil]]
	- [[tadalafil]]
- riociguat
- Lung transplant
- Atrial septostomy
- Potts shunt

Complications:
- [[Pulmonary Embolism|PE]]
- [[Pulmonary Edema]]
- Pulmonary hemorrhage
