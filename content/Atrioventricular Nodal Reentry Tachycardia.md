Most common type of [[Supraventricular Tachycardia|SVT]] in people without structural heart abnormality

From reentry circuit in or around [[Atrioventricular Node|AV Node]]

Characteristics:
- Usually paroxysmal
	- Can be triggered by exertion, caffeine, or alcohol
- Can have palpitations and brief hypotension
- If [[Coronary Artery Disease|CAD]], can have chest pain
- Can have SOB, anxiety, polyuria from elevated atrial pressure releasing atrial natriuretic peptide
- HR 140-280
- Regular rhythm

Slow-Fast AVNRT (80-90%):
- Antegrade [[P-Wave]]:
	- hidden in QRS OR
	- forms pseudo r' wave in V1 AND/OR
	- forms pseudo S wave in leads II, III, aVF

Fast-Slow AVNRT (10%):
- Antegrade [[P-Wave]] between QRS and T wave

Slow-Slow AVNRT (1.5%)
- Difficult to differentiate with sinus tachycardia
