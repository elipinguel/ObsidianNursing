Measured with [[Pulmonary Artery Catheter]]

![[PA Catheter Waveforms.png]]

Spontaneous inhalation causes a dip in pressure
Systolic occurs in the middle of T wave

Normal: 15-30 / 5-15

Systolic:
- Elevated:
	- [[Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)|COPD]]
	- [[Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS)|ARDS]]
	- [[Pulmonary Hypertension]]
	- [[Pulmonary Embolism (PE)|PE]]
	- [[Mitral Regurgitation]]
	- [[Mitral stenosis]]
	- Low left ventricular compliance
	- Aortic valve regurgitation
- Lowered:
	- Pulmonary valve regurgitation
Diastolic:
- Elevated:
	- [[Cardiac Tamponade]]
	- [[Mitral stenosis]]
	- Left ventricle failure
	- Low left ventricular compliance
	- Aortic valve regurgitation
- Lowered:
	- [[Right Bundle Branch Block]] due to delay in right ventricle contraction
	- Pulmonary valve regurgitation
