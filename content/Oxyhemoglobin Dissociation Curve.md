> [!tip]
> Right releases
> Left loves

Right shift lowers [[Hemoglobin Oxygen Saturation|SaO2]] due to tissues needing more oxygen
Left shift increases [[Hemoglobin Oxygen Saturation|SaO2]]

Right shift causes:
- Acidosis
- ↑CO2
- ↑temperature
- ==Increase in 2,3-diphosphoglycerate==
	- From tissue hypoxia
	- Reduces [[Hemoglobin]] binding to increase tissue oxygenation
Left shift causes:
- ↓temperature
- Alkalosis
- ↓CO2
