Created in [[Hypothalamus]] and stored in [[Neurohypophysis]]

- Increases water reabsorption in [[Distal Convoluted Tubule]]
- Vasoconstriction through V1 receptor after 15 mins

Released when ↑[[Osmolality]], anesthesia, analgesicsm stress, pain

.>0.04 units/min causes severe ischemia and altered [[Platelet]] function

Monitor for cardiac ischemia
