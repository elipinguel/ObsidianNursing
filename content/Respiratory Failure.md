PaO2 < PaCo2

Types:
- Hypoxic
	- PaO2 < 60
	- Refractory to supplemental oxygen
- Hypercapnic
	- PaCO2 > 50
	- PaO2 improves with supplemental oxygen
- Perioperative
	- Incentive spirometer
	- Controlled pain
	- Abdominal splint
- Secondary to shock

Signs and symptoms:
- Severe morning headache
- Tachycardia and hypertension → bradycardia and hypotension
- Retractions
- Tripod position
- Pursed lip breathing
- Guppy breathing
- Narrow or reversed [[Inspiratory-Expiratory Ratio]]

Treatment:
- 2-3 L/day due to respiratory water loss
- High protein, high fat, low carbohydrate
	- Carbohydrates break down into CO2
- #corticosteroid 
- #bronchodilator 
- #diuretic , #nitrate if needed
