Causes:
- Post-op cardiac surgery

Signs:
- stabbing chest pain
	- relief when leaning forward
	- radiates to trapezium ridge
	- increased w/ inspiration
- low-grade fever
- [[Kussmaul's Sign]]
- [[Pericardial Friction Rub]]
- [[Jugular Vein Distention (JVD)|JVD]]
- tachycardia
- pallor
- ==**ST elevation in all leads**==
- [[Cardiac Tamponade]]

Treatment:
- #NSAID especially [[aspirin]]
