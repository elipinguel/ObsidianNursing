Total calcium > 10.5 or ionized calcium > 5.6 mg/dL (1.3 mmol/L)

Causes:
- [[Chronic Kidney Disease]]
- Prolonged immobility causing leaching from bones

Signs and symptoms:
- Hypertension
- Hypotension
- Bradycardia
- AV block
- Bundle branch block
- Shortened QT interval
- Flattened or inverted T wave
- [[Digitalis Toxicity]]
- Renal calculi
- Seizures
- Weakness

Treatment:
- Titrate [[NS]] for 2-3 mL/kg/hr urine output
- [[Dialysis]]
- #loop_diuretic 
- chelating agents
- [[calcitonin]] to increase renal excretion
- #bisphosphonate to decrease bone resorption
- #corticosteroid to decrease GI calcium absorption
- Oral phosphates to bind to GI calcium
