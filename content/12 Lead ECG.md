Lateral Wall:
- I, aVL, V5, V6
- Should be positive. If positive then negative, then moving away from left side and thus [[Right Axis Deviation]]
Inferior Wall:
- II, II, aVF
- ST depression in aVL is suspicious for inferior MI
- Should be positive. If positive then negative, then moving away from bottom left side and thus [[Left Axis Deviation]]
- Infarction causes 2nd degree type 1 [[Atrioventricular Block|AV Block]]
Septum:
- V1, V2
Anterior wall:
- V1, V2, V3, V4
- No R wave indicates infarcted (if no ST elevation) or infarcting (if ST elevation)
- Infarction causes 2nd degree type 2 [[Atrioventricular Block|AV Block]]
Right ventricle:
- V4R
	- Placed on 5th intercostal space, right midclavicular line
Posterior Wall:
- V7, V8, V9
	- ST elevation may be hidden due to electricity poorly conducting through the lungs
Left main coronary artery:
- aVR

All V leads have R waves. R waves should increase in size from V1 to V6
