>[!tip]
Non-selective drugs includes letters N-Z, labetalol, and carvedilol and should NOT be used in lung disease ([[Asthma]], [[Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease|COPD]])

Indications:
- [[Hypertension|HTN]]
- [[Heart Failure with Reduced Ejection Fraction|HFrEF]]
- [[Myocardial Infarction|MI]]
- [[Coronary Artery Disease|CAD]]

- [[metoprolol]]
- [[esmolol]]
- [[labetalol]]
- [[carvedilol]]
- [[propranolol]]

Overdose treatment:
- [[atropine]]
	- Reverse blockage of [[Beta-1 Receptor]]
- [[glucagon]]
	- Reverse blockage of [[Beta-2 Receptor]]
- [[Calcium]] to improve contractility
	- Reverses blockage of [[Beta-1 Receptor]]
