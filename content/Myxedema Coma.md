Severe hypothyroidism

Signs and symptoms:
- AMS
- Hypotension
- Hypothermia
- Dilutional [[Hyponatremia]]
- Hypoglycemia
- Hypoventilation → respiratory acidosis
- Low T3 and T4
- Dysrhythmias

Treatment:
- [[levothyroxine]] and [[liothyronine]]
- Mechanical ventilation
- Blankets
	- Avoid warming devices as it causes vasodilation and hypotension
- Careful fluids or vasopressors
- [[hydrocortisone]] to test if adrenal insufficiency is the cause
