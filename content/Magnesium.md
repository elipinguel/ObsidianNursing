# Magnesium sulfate

Antidote: [[Calcium]]

Contraindications:
- [[Myasthenia Gravis]]

Adverse effects:
- Diaphoresis
- Flushing
- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Headache
- Weakness
- Visual disturbances
- Dyspnea
	- Indicates pulmonary edema
- No deep tendon reflexes
	- Indicates magnesium levels 7-10 mEq/L
