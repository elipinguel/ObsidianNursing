Signs and symptoms:
- [[Jaundice]]
- ↑Unconjugated bilirubin
- [[Portal Hypertension]]

Avoid:
- [[Hypokalemia]]
	- Kidneys retain potassium and ammonium ions
- [[Azotemia]]
	- Urea is reverted into ammonia by gut microbia
- Protein
	- Protein metabolism produces ammonia
	- Gut microbe metabolism of protein produces ammonia
		- Includes blood from GI bleed
- [[Lactated Ringers|LR]]
	- Unhealthy liver cannot convert lactate into bicarb, causing acidosis
- Acidosis
