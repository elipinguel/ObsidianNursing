Inflammatory #cytokine

Secreted by:
- Macrophages
- [[Osteoblast]]
- 

Increased by:
- Infection
- Burns
- Autoimmune conditions

Effects:
- [[Iron]] sequestration and reduced iron absorption
	- Increases [[Hepcidin]] and [[Ferritin]]
- ↑[[C-Reactive Protein|CRP]], ↑[[Fibrinogen]], ↑[[Thrombopoietin]]
- ↑[[Osteoclast]], ↑[[T Cell]], ↑[[B Cell]] activity
- ↓[[albumin]]
