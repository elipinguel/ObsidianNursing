Small deflection immediately after [[T Wave]]. Size is inversely proportional to heart rate

Usually the same direction as [[T Wave]]

Best seen in V2 and V3


Causes:
- Delayed Purkinje fiber repolarization
- Prolonged mid-myocardial "M-cells" repolarization
- After-potential from mechanical forces on ventricular wall

Abnormalities:
- [[Prominent U Wave]]
- [[Inverted U Wave]]
