Early:
- Bronchoconstriction
- Airway edema
- ↓low PaCo2
Late:
- Airway inflammation
- ↑PaCO2

Signs and symptoms:
- Wheezing is variable
	- Look at neurological status
- Air trapping
- ↓[[Inspiratory-Expiratory Ratio]]

Treatment:
- #bronchodilator 
	- Short-acting
		- [[albuterol]]
		- For rescue
	- Long-acting + #corticosteroid 
		- Better for long-term management
- #biologic
- #muscarinic_antagonist
