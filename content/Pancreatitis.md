Causes:
- Pancreatic duct obstruction
	- Gallstones, infection
- Alcoholism
- #cyclosporine, #corticosteroid, #thiazide_diuretic, #tetracycline
- Trauma

Autodigestion of pancreas

Signs and symptoms:
- [[Hypocalcemia]]
	- Due to enzymes binding to serum calcium
- [[Hyperglycemic Hyperosmolar State|HHS]]
	- Due to low insulin production
- Left-sided atelectasis and pleural effusion
- Bilateral lung crackles
	- Due to systemic inflammation
		- Can lead to [[Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS)|ARDS]]
- [[Cullen Sign]]
- [[Grey Turner's Sign]]
- Elevated amylase and lipase
