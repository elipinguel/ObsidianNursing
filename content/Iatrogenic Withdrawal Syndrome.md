Withdrawal from discontinuation of medications such as #opioid and sedatives after being used for days

#### #Opioid:
- Preventions:
	- Start by tapering by 10% per day
	- Add multimodal approach:
		- [[acetaminophen]], [[ketamine]], [[gabapentin]]
- Treatment:
	- Oral [[methadone]] 24 hrs prior to stopping IV [[fentanyl]]
	- Oral [[clonidine]] to manage withdrawal symptoms
#### [[dexmedetomidine]]:
- Usually develops after 3 days of continuous infusion
- Treatment:
	- Oral [[clonidine]] since both are #alpha-2_agonist 
#### [[propofol]]:
- Little documented evidence of withdrawal
- Treatment:
	- [[phenobarbital]] for refractory withdrawal symptoms
