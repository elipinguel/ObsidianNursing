Causes:
- Chronic alcoholism
- Autoimmune
- Genetic

Signs and symptoms:
- Chronic, gnawing abdominal pain
- Inability to digest fats
	- [[Steatorrhea]]
	- Weight loss
	- Vitamin deficiency
- Diabetes mellitus

Treatment:
- Non-opioid long-acting analgesia
	- Pain is usually neuropathic
- Pancreatic enzyme replacement therapy (PERT)
	- [[pancrelipase]]
- Low-fat meals
- Fat-soluble vitamins
- **Alcohol cessation**
- Insulin
