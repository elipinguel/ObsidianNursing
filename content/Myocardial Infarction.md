aliases:
  - MI
---
Signs and Symptoms:
- Upper abdominal pain
	- Usually women
- Chest pain

Increase oxygen delivery:
- [[nitroglycerin]]
- [[aspirin]]
- [[clopidogrel]], prasugrel, ticagrelor, cangrelor
- Oxygen **ONLY** if SpO2 <90% due to ischemic reperfusion cascade
Decrease [[Oxygen Consumption]]:
- [[nitroglycerin]]
- #beta_blocker 
- [[morphine]] for pain
- #ACE_inhibitor or #ARB for [[Ejection Fraction]] < 40%

Metrics:
- Door to Door (PCI capable facility): 45 mins
- Door to needle: 30 mins (thrombolytics)
- Door to balloon: 90 mins (PCA)
- Onset to ECG: 10 mins
- Door to ECG: 10 mins

Post MI complications:
- [[Papillary Muscle Rupture]]
- [[Free Wall Rupture]]
- [[Cardiogenic Shock]]
- [[Ventricular Aneurysm]]
- [[Dressler's Syndrome]]
