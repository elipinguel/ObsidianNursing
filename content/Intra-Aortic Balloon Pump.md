==Deflated during systolic; Inflated during diastole==

Purpose:
- Decrease afterload
- Increase coronary perfusion
- Improve [[Cardiac Output (CO)|CO]]
- Decrease myocardial oxygen demand

Contraindications:
- ==[[Aortic Insufficiency|Aortic Regurgitation]]==
- [[Peripheral Vascular Disease|PVD]] due to risk for breaking clot

Complications:
- Limb ischemia
- Balloon leak
- Pulse deficit
- AMS
- Renal infarct

Weaning:
- 1:1 to 1:2 support

Removal criteria:
- ==[[Pulmonary Artery Wedge Pressure|PAWP]] < 18==
- Minimal pressors

Do not sit patient too high lest kink catheter
