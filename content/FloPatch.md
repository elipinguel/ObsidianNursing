Wireless, wearable doppler ultrasound of the carotid artery

Initiation requirements:
1. Indication for fluids
	1. hypotension, oliguria, long capillary refill
2. Not fluid overloaded
3. Measure carotid corrected flow time with FloPatch

Usage:
1. Place on carotid artery
2. Measure carotid corrected flow time
3. Do [[Passive Leg Raise]] or [[Fluid Challenge]]
4. ≥7 increase in flow time indicates fluid responsiveness
5. Give fluids
	1. If still needing fluids, repeat initiation requirements.
