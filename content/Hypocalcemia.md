Total calcium < 9 or ionized calcium < 4.5 mg/dL (1.05 mmol/L)

Causes:
- [[citrate]] in blood products bind to calcium
- [[Alkalosis]]
- [[Chronic Kidney Disease|CKD]]
- [[Rhabdomyolysis]]

Signs and symptoms:
- Hypotension
- Prolonged QT interval
- Bradycardia
- V-tach
- Weakness
- Hyperreflexia
- [[Chvostek Sign]]
- [[Trousseau Sign]]
- Seizure

Treatment:
- [[Vitamin D]] to stimulate [[Calcium]] absorption and distal tubule reabsorption
- Calcium
