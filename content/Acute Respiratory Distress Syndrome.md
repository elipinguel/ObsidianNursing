---
aliases:
  - ARDS
---
Causes:
- Pneumonia
- Drowning
- [[Sepsis]]
- Trauma
- Inhalation injury
- Reperfusion injury
- [[Transfusion-Related Acute Lung Injury]]

Criteria:
- Lung injury w/in 7 days of known risk factor
- Cannot be explained by cardiogenic pulmonary edema or volume overload
- Whiteout lung not explained by lung disease
- SpO2/FiO2 ratio ≤315 with SpO2 ≤97%

Types:
- Mild
	- PF ratio 201-300
- Moderate
	- PF ratio101-200
- Severe
	- PF ratio<101

Pathophysiology:
1. Exudative phase
	1. Loss of type II pneumocytes and loss of surfactant
	2. Hyaline membrane formation
	3. Hypoxic vasoconstriction
2. Fibroproliferative phase
	1. within 3-10 days
3. Resolution
	1. Fibrosis with scarring and cyst

Treatment:
1.  Non-invasive ventilation
2. ↑[[Positive End-Expiratory Pressure|PEEP]]
3. ↓[[Tidal Volume|TV]]
4. Prone
	1. Only severe ARDS
	2. No time to wait for pronning bed. Prone ASAP
	3. At least 16 hrs
	4. Contraindications:
		1. Unstable spine
		2. ↑[[Intracranial Pressure|ICP]]
	5. Avoid:
		1. Unstable chest wall
		2. Unstable pelvic or long bone fracture
		3. 160 kg or greater
		4. Hemodynamically unstable
