Static indicator of fluid responsiveness

100-250 mL IV rapid fluid bolus over with pressure bag 10 mins.

>[!warning]
>999 mL/hr on the infusion pump will only deliver 166 mL in 10 mins. A pressure bag is required

Patient is responsive to fluids if:
- >10% increase in [[Stroke Volume]] **OR**
- >15% increase in [[Cardiac Index]]
after 90 seconds
