Cumulative blood loss ≥1000 mL after birth OR blood loss with hypovolemia within 24 hrs of birth.

Risk factors:
- Prior c-section
- Low lying placenta
- Multiple gestation
- Chorioamnionitis

Preventions:
- ==[[oxytocin]]==
- fundal massage
- clamping the cord
- inspection of placenta

Stages:
- 0
	- Blood loss <1000 mL c-section OR <500 mL vaginal
	- Normal vital signs and labs
	- **Not considered postpartum hemorrhage**
- 1
	- Blood loss >1000 mL c-section OR >500 mL vaginal delivery
	- Normal vital signs and labs
	- Interventions
		- Consider [[misoprostol]], [[tranexamic acid]], [[methylergonovine]] or [[carboprost]]
		- Confirm blood type
		- Determine source of bleeding
		- Bimanual massage
		- Indwelling urinary catheter
		- Monitor quantified blood loss
		- Call for assistance
		- Increase [[oxytocin]] rate
- 2
	- Blood loss up to 1500 mL or 2 uterotonic administrations in addition to [[oxytocin]]
	- Interventions:
		- Draw CBC, CMP, coagulation panel, [[Thromboelastography|TEG]], [[Fibrinogen]], type and cross
		- ≥2 large bore IVs
		- Prepare for operating room (OR)
		- Transfuse 2 units [[Packed Red Blood Cells|PRBCs]]. Do not wait for labs.
- 3
	- Blood loss >1500 mL OR 2 units [[Packed Red Blood Cells|PRBCs]] administered OR risk for occult bleeding/coagulopathy OR abnormal vital signs/labs/oliguria
		- Tachycardia
		- Tachypnea
		- Narrow pulse pressure
		- Anxiety
		- Shock index >0.9
	- Interventions:
		- Initiate [[Massive Transfusion Protocol]]
- 4
	- Cardiovascular collapse
	- Interventions:
		- Hysterectomy
		- ACLS algorithm
		- [[Massive Transfusion Protocol]]

Interventions:
- Lower head of bead
- Warm blanket to upper torso
- [[Platelet]]/[[Cryoprecipitate]] for coagulopathy
- [[oxytocin]] for uterine atony
- Sutures for laceration
- Cord traction or manual removal for retained tissue

Measuring quantified blood loss:
- Weigh blood-soaked materials before and after blood and convert grams to mL
- Avoid estimating
- Report cumulative blood loss to team members
