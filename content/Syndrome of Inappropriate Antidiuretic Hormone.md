> [!caution] Never give hypotonic solutions
> [[D5W]] is isotonic but becomes hypotonic as glucose is metabolized!

Too much [[vasopressin|ADH]]

Causes:
- Small cell lung cancer
- [[Pneumonia]]
- CNS insult
- Anesthesia
- Analgesics

Signs and symptoms:
- [[Hyponatremia]]
- [[Osmolality]] < 285 mOsm/L
- ↑[[Specific Gravity]]

Treatment:
- Fluid restriction
- [[Hyponatremia]] treatment
