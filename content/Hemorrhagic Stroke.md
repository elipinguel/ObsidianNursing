Signs and Symptoms:
- Decreased [[Level of Consciousness|LOC]]
- Pupil changes
- Motor changes
- Headache

Risk factors:
- [[Hypertension|HTN]]

Types:
- Intracerebral hemorrhage (ICH)
	- Usually in the [[Basal Ganglia]]
	- .>30 mL bleed → consider goals of care
- ![[Subarachnoid Hemorrhage]]
- Intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH)
	- Caused by ICH or SAH
	- Treat with 1 mg [[alteplase]] intraventricular Q8 hrs
- Transformation of ischemic bleed
	- Stop [[alteplase]]
	- BP control

BP Control:

| ICH      | [[Subarachnoid Hemorrhage]] |                                                                      |
| -------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|          | Pre-securement                    | Post-securement                                                      |
| SBP ≤140 | SBP ≤160                          | SBP ≥160 to prevent delayed cerebral ischemia (DCI) due to vasospasm |
Lower with IV push [[labetalol]] or [[nicardipine]] drip
