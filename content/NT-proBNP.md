Inactive byproduct of [[Brain Natriuretic Peptide|BNP]] production

Half-life of 120 mins

Possible [[Heart Failure]] if
- >450 pg/mL age <50
- > 900 pg/mL age 50-75
- >1800 pg/mL age >75

Not [[Heart Failure]] if <300 pg/mL

>[!tip]
>Decreases with obesity.
>Increases with age, [[Chronic Kidney Disease|CKD]], cardiac stress.
