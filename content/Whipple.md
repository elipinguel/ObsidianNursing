Remove pancreatic cancer and reconstruct digestive tract

Adverse effects:
- Pancreatic leak → gastric-duodenal artery hemorrhage
	- Treat with angiography with stenting/embolization

Post-Op:
- Antibiotics
	- Reduces re-bleed since infection causes endogenous heparin release
- NG tube for 24 hrs
- Amylase levels from abdominal drain on post-op days 1 and 3
