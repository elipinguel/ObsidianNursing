Secondary to a primary condition

Excessive clotting → ischemia; use up all clotting factors → bleed

Rapid clot lysis → ↑fibrin split products → bleed

Signs and symptoms:
- ↓fibrinogen
- ↑PT ↑aPTT
- ↑Fibrin split products (FSP)
- D-Dimer < 500 ng/mL
- [[Platelet]] < 100,000

Treatment:
- Treat underlying cause
- [[heparin]]
- Once PT and aPTT are normal for heparin, start [[Fresh Frozen Plasma]] and [[Cryoprecipitate]]
- [[Platelet]]  transfusion if < 20,000
