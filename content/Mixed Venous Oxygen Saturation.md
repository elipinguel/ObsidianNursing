Percentage of oxygen

Probe in pulmonary artery

5-10% lower than [[Central Venous Oxygen Saturation]]

Values:
- .>80%
	- Cells not utilizing oxygen
- ==76-80% is normal==
- 51-75%
	- Increasing oxygen demand
- 31-50%
	- Start of lactic acidosis
- 25-30%
	- Severe lactic acidosis
- <25%
	- Death
