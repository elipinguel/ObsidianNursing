Predicts mortality in [[Subarachnoid Hemorrhage]]

Higher = more comatose

Scale:

| Signs and Symptoms                                                     | Score |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| Mild headache<br>Alert and oriented<br>No/Minimal nuchal rigidity      | +1    |
| Moderate-Severe headache<br>Alert and oriented<br>Full nuchal rigidity | +2    |
| Lethargy or confusion<br>Mild focal deficit                            | +3    |
| Stuporous<br>Severe focal deficit                                      | +4    |
| Comatose<br>Severe neurological impairment                             | +5    |
