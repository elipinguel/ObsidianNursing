[[Liver Disease]] → [[Portal Hypertension]] → Blood backing up in spleen → Splanchnic arterial vasodilation → Effective arterial hypovolemia → [[Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System|RAAS]] → Renal artery vasoconstriction → [[Prerenal Acute Kidney Injury|Prerenal AKI]]

Signs and symptoms:
- Urine sodium < 10 mEq/L
- Urine osmolality > 500 mOsm/kg

Treatment:
- [[albumin]]
- [[midodrine]] to vasoconstrict gut
- [[octreotide]] to reduce splanchnic blood flow
- Liver transplant
- [[Dialysis]]

Avoid:
- #NSAID 
- #aminoglycoside
- Contrast
- #diuretic
